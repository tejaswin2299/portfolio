Workshop Three focus

This artifact connects deep-learning concepts with the human challenge of adapting to new realities. Neural networks improve through repeated examples, feedback, weight updates, and error correction. In a similar way, effective leaders learn from outcomes, adjust their approach, communicate clearly, and continue moving toward a goal without pretending every first attempt will succeed.

Learning goal

Explain ANN, CNN, RNN, GAN, and activation functions in clear language.

Leadership goal

Connect feedback, resilience, communication, and change leadership to AI/ML integration.

Ethical goal

Recognize the value and risks of synthetic data, including manipulation and deepfakes.

Deep-learning concepts

ANN

Connected layers learn weighted patterns from examples and adjust those weights to reduce error.

CNN

Filters detect local visual patterns such as edges, shapes, and textures, making CNNs effective for images.

RNN

Information from earlier steps influences later steps, supporting sequence tasks such as text or time-series analysis.

GAN

A generator and discriminator compete, which can improve synthetic outputs but also create misuse risks.

Activation functions: ReLU helps hidden layers learn nonlinear patterns efficiently, while softmax converts output scores into probabilities across several classes.

Artifact reflection

I selected this artifact because it brings together the technical and leadership themes of the workshop. Neural networks improve by processing examples, measuring error, and adjusting internal weights. That process mirrors adaptive leadership because leaders also need feedback, patience, communication, and a willingness to revise a plan when conditions change. Faith strengthens this approach by encouraging humility, perseverance, and responsibility rather than fear when a new challenge appears.

The Python evidence helped me move beyond definitions and observe how an activation function, hidden layer, normalized inputs, and repeated training affect classification. I also learned that a strong accuracy score does not remove ethical responsibility. CNNs, RNNs, and GANs can create useful solutions, but synthetic media and deepfakes can mislead people. My leadership approach is therefore to explain the purpose, verify data and outputs, involve affected users, and keep human review in decisions that may cause harm.
