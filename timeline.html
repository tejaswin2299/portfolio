Why this artifact matters

This timeline turns AI history into an interactive learning experience. It was selected because understanding the field’s cycles of progress, disappointment, renewed investment, and public impact helps me evaluate current claims more carefully.

Purpose

Explain important AI and machine-learning milestones in a visual, accessible format.

Audience

Students, instructors, technical peers, and hiring managers who want a quick historical overview.

Skills shown

Research synthesis, concise writing, information design, HTML, CSS, JavaScript, and source review.

Evidence of process

Research

I compared milestone dates and descriptions, then shortened the content so each event could be understood quickly.

Design decision

I used a circuit-board visual language because it connects computation, hardware, and the historical development of intelligent systems.

Quality control

I reviewed the final text, tested keyboard navigation and responsive behavior, and retained a disclosure of how AI supported organization and troubleshooting.

Evidence: live timeline, source list inside the original page, and the HTML/CSS/JavaScript implementation.

Artifact reflection

I selected this artifact because it demonstrates that I can organize a large amount of technical history into a focused experience for different audiences. Building the timeline required more than collecting dates. I had to decide which milestones mattered, explain their impact in plain language, and design an interface that encouraged exploration instead of presenting another long document.

The project taught me that AI progress is not a straight line. Periods of high expectations, limited results, improved data, stronger computing, and renewed investment repeatedly changed the field. That pattern reminds me to approach new AI claims with curiosity and evidence, communicate limitations honestly, and remain adaptable when tools or assumptions change.
