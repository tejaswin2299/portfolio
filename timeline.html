Artifact reflection

Why this timeline belongs in my professional portfolio

I selected this artifact because it combines research, technical communication, interface design, and front-end development. Instead of presenting AI history as a static list, I created an interactive experience in which each milestone functions like a component on a circuit board. This format makes a complex subject easier to explore and demonstrates how I organize information for a specific audience.

Audience and purpose

The main audience includes instructors, classmates, recruiters, and professionals who want a clear overview of how artificial intelligence developed. The short milestone summaries support non-specialists, while contributor and impact details provide enough depth for technical readers. The terminal and circuit-board interface also connects the historical content with the computing theme.

Skills demonstrated

The artifact demonstrates source review, content selection, information architecture, HTML, CSS, JavaScript, modal interaction, responsive layout, accessibility considerations, and concise explanation of technical history. It also shows my ability to turn a broad research topic into a structured digital product.

What I learned

I learned that the strongest timeline is not the one with the largest number of dates. Each event must explain why the milestone changed the field. I also learned to balance visual creativity with usability: the interface needs to feel distinctive without making the content difficult to read or navigate.

Future improvement

In a future version, I would add category filters, keyboard shortcuts for moving between milestones, a compact comparison of symbolic and data-driven AI eras, and a clearly dated update section for new developments. I would also conduct usability testing with both technical and nontechnical users.
